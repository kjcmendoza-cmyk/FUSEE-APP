Laboratory Guidelines & Protocols

1. Safety & Proper Attire

All EE students entering D202, D204, or D205 laboratories must wear proper attire (closed shoes, long pants, ID badge visible). Food and open liquids are strictly prohibited near workstations and testing benches.

2. Borrowing Duration & Overdue Policies

Equipment borrowed via the Tool Management System must be returned within 3 working days unless granted an extended permit by the laboratory custodian or course professor.

3. Mandatory Digital Logging

Students must scan/log in using their student credentials on the respective logbook portal BEFORE touching laboratory PCs or powering bench circuits.

Computer Lab Workstation Rules

D202 PC Usage Standards

• Saving files locally on lab PCs is prohibited; use cloud storage or flash drives.

• Log out of your digital session immediately upon ending your workstation time.

Hardware & Circuits Safety

D205 Safety Checklist

• Verify power supply voltages with a multimeter before connecting sensitive ICs.

• Turn off main bench circuit breakers prior to re-wiring high-current components.

• Wear protective eye glasses when soldering or trimming lead wires.
