EE Laboratory Policies

Official AdDU SEA EE Rules

1. General Safety & Protocol

All EE students entering D202, D204, or D205 laboratories must wear proper attire (closed shoes, long pants, ID badge visible). Food and open liquids are strictly prohibited near workstations and testing benches.

2. Borrowing Limits & Duration

Equipment borrowed via the Tool Management System must be returned within 3 working days unless granted an extended permit by the laboratory custodian or course professor.

3. Mandatory Digital Logging

Students must scan/log in using their student credentials on the respective logbook portal BEFORE touching laboratory PCs or powering bench circuits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for EE students

How do I borrow tools for home/overnight thesis use?

Submit a request via the Tool Management System student link, then present your validated AdDU ID to the lab custodian in D204 along with your course instructor's approval slip.

What if I forget to log out of the computer lab?

Sessions auto-close at 7:00 PM daily. However, failing to manually log out creates active duration flags on your account. Always click "Log Out" on the digital logbook before leaving workstation.

Can non-EE engineering students use the hardware logbook?

Yes, provided they are enrolled in an interdisciplinary circuit/electronics course (e.g., ECE, ME, ChE) and have registered their student number in the system.

Hardware & Circuits Safety

D205 Safety Checklist

• Verify power supply voltages with a multimeter before connecting sensitive ICs.

• Turn off main bench circuit breakers prior to re-wiring high-current components.

• Wear protective eye glasses when soldering or trimming lead wires.
